# Syllabus data notes

Source portal: https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/e-syllabus

The official IBASS portal and its published syllabus materials were queried for the 2026-10 build. The topic headings and section organization below were checked against the official subject PDFs downloaded through the IBASS portal. The bundled text is a compact topic checklist, not the full syllabus content/objectives.

- English Language (shown as "Use of English" in the PDF): verified the three syllabus sections and the core listed areas for Comprehension and Summary, Lexis and Structure, and Oral Forms. The checklist paraphrases/combines some PDF subtopics into study-friendly tasks; "approved reading text" is a checklist reminder and the actual prescribed title can change. Check the current official PDF for that title and full objectives.
- Mathematics: verified all five official sections and the numbered topic headings in Number and Numeration, Algebra, Geometry/Trigonometry, Calculus and Statistics. The checklists summarize subtopic coverage under those headings, rather than reproduce every objective or note verbatim.
- Physics: verified the 40 numbered topic headings against the official PDF. The checklist includes these headings and in a few instances groups closely related wording; it does not reproduce every subtopic/objective.
- Chemistry: verified all 17 numbered topic headings against the official PDF. The checklist uses the headings only, without reproducing detailed content and objectives.

Assumptions and limits: several checklist labels are concise paraphrases for usability, not JAMB's exact full syllabus wording. For English and Mathematics, some tasks combine multiple listed subtopics. This is not a claim that every note, objective, recommended text, or later syllabus amendment is included. Consult IBASS for the full and latest authoritative materials before relying on this checklist for exam preparation.

The official syllabus documents and wording are JAMB materials. This project links to IBASS and includes limited headings/paraphrases for a personal study aid; it does not claim ownership of JAMB content or grant a license to redistribute official documents. The MIT license applies to the original application code only.

## Practice question bank

`questions.js` contains 520 original practice questions, with 5 MCQs for each of the 104 syllabus topics. All topics are covered:

- English Language: 20 of 20 topics (100 questions).
- Mathematics: 27 of 27 topics (135 questions).
- Physics: 40 of 40 topics (200 questions).
- Chemistry: 17 of 17 topics (85 questions).

Topic keys in `questions.js` use each subject's section and topic indexes from `syllabus.js`. The quizzes are original topic-aligned practice written for this app, not official JAMB past questions and not copied from JAMB materials. Questions target core topic concepts, and are not a substitute for the full syllabus or exam-preparation materials. A topic displays a Practice button only after it is marked studied and has its five-question set.
